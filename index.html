Vial Math

The 940 vials at 50 vials a box take 19 boxes, the last box holding 40 vials.

Boundary: arithmetic only Vial Math counts a lab's sample vial stock with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the vials take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a vial, and the minutes the labeling takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your vials, your boxes or your bench and does not know whether the vials will really arrive unbroken, a cap will cross-thread or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a vial is right for your samples. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 940 vials, 50 vials a box, 110 vials a week, $27.40 a box and 12 vials a minute.

The weeks truth

The 940 vials at 110 vials a week last 8 full weeks with 60 vials left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 19 boxes at $27.40 a box come to $520.60, $0.55 a vial.

The time truth

The 940 vials at 12 vials a minute take 79 minutes, 1 hour 19 minutes.
