Vial Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a lab's sample vial stock with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the boxes the vials take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a vial, and the minutes the labeling takes. It uses no named constants. The vials, the box size, the weekly use, the box price and the labeling speed are yours to decide and read off your own inventory, logs and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your vials, your boxes or your bench and does not know whether the vials will really arrive unbroken, a cap will cross-thread or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a box is worth its price or a vial is right for your samples. Every number here is your own entry.

The boxes truth

Boxes: the vials divided by the vials a box, rounded up, with what the last box holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the vials divided by the vials used a week, with the vials left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the boxes times the box price, to the cent, and spread over the vials.

The time truth

Time: the vials divided by the vials labeled a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Vial Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
